## 1. Objective
To build a clickable, production-grade high-fidelity mobile prototype of MalhotraUPI with rich visual cues, refined design system tokens, realistic transaction states, and interactive user micro-interactions.

---

## 2. Visual Design System

### A. Color Palette
- **Primary Trust Blue (`#1D61E7`):** Instills confidence, reliability, and security for primary action buttons, total balance cards, and focus states.
- **Background Slate (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft, neutral background preventing eye strain in high-glare environments.
- **Surface Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-elevation cards providing clear grouping and spatial separation.
- **Success Green (`#16A34A`):** Used for instant visual confirmation ticks and positive transaction inflows (`+₹2,400`).
- **Text & Grays (`#0F172A`, `#64748B`):** Strict high-contrast typography meeting WCAG AA accessibility standards.

### B. Typography & Iconography
- **Font Family:** Modern Sans-Serif with generous letter spacing and line heights for monetary values.
- **Iconography:** Universally understood stroke icons with accompanying textual labels to support both tech-savvy and semi-literate users.

### C. Layout & Micro-Interactions
- Floating bottom navigation with a prominent circular QR scan trigger positioned for single-thumb reachability.
- Subtle card elevation and tactile button-press feedback states.
- Modal sheet payment confirmation displaying verified merchant/peer credentials before funds are debited.

---

## 3. Evaluated Interactive User Flows
1. **QR Payment Flow:** Dashboard $\rightarrow$ Tap Floating Scan $\rightarrow$ Camera Scanner overlay $\rightarrow$ Amount Entry $\rightarrow$ Success Checkmark Sheet.
2. **Direct Contact Payment:** Dashboard $\rightarrow$ Send Money $\rightarrow$ Select Recent Contact $\rightarrow$ Amount Chips $\rightarrow$ Review $\rightarrow$ Payment Confirmation.
3. **Transaction Inspection:** Dashboard $\rightarrow$ History Tab $\rightarrow$ Spending Analysis $\rightarrow$ Tap Transaction Row $\rightarrow$ Full Transaction Receipt with Reference Number.
4. **Account & Safety:** Dashboard $\rightarrow$ Profile $\rightarrow$ Security & Privacy Settings $\rightarrow$ Linked Bank Overview.
