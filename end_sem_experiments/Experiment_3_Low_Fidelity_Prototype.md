## 1. Objective
To design and validate the initial architectural layout, screen hierarchy, and interaction flows of MalhotraUPI using low-fidelity wireframes before committing to high-fidelity visual styling.

---

## 2. Key Design Considerations
1. **Focus on Primary Payment Tasks:** Ensure Scan & Pay and Send Money are immediately discoverable on the main viewport.
2. **Minimal Cognitive Load:** Remove promotional banners, gaming features, and loan advertisements that clutter competitors like Paytm.
3. **Streamlined Navigation:** Keep a flat structure with a 4-tab bottom navigation bar (`Home`, `Send`, `History`, `Profile`) and a central quick-access Scan action button.
4. **Transparent Transaction Review:** Introduce an explicit pre-payment review screen displaying the recipient's verified name and UPI ID to prevent transfer errors.

---

## 3. Wireframed Screen Flows
- **Frame 01 — Home Dashboard:** Features total balance summary card, linked primary bank account indicator, prominent action cards (`Scan & Pay`, `Send Money`, `Request`), and recent transactions.
- **Frame 02 — Send Money Flow:** Clean input field for UPI ID / mobile number, recent contact shortcuts (`Arjun`, `Priya`, `Rohan`), quick amount chips (`+₹500`, `+₹1,000`, `+₹2,000`), optional note field, and security tip banner.
- **Frame 03 — Review & Confirmation:** Clean breakdown of recipient details, debit account, transfer amount, and primary 'Pay Securely' trigger.
- **Frame 04 — Success State:** Unambiguous green checkmark, payment confirmation chime cue, transaction ID with one-tap copy, and direct 'Back to Home' navigation.
- **Frame 05 — Transaction History:** Monthly spending summary card (`Money sent`, `Money received`, `Total transactions`), search bar, and segmented filter tabs (`All`, `Sent`, `Received`).
- **Frame 06 — Profile & Account Settings:** User identity verification badge, contact details, security & privacy controls, and linked bank management.
